Inside the build

Rules honored, details carried

  • Type scale Tool: 24 · 20 · 16 · 13 · 11. Wall: 72 · 24 · 18 · 14 · 12. Nothing else — the 31-size zoo is gone by construction.
  • One meaning per position Left spine = kit membership only. Deviance is a red chip in the row's own lane; the floor note is the 📌 text; age lives on the band's right edge.
  • Constant row anatomy Every row is one height (64px tool / 46px wall) — count, note and deviance fit inside the second text line instead of ballooning the row.
  • Everything ambient behind one bar ▤ Today · 41 out · 57 in — tap to open shipped/received/feed on the tool. The wall shows them permanently; that difference IS the two roles.
  • Carried from the old board 44px ✓ Pick · beacon slot (unchanged behavior) · pack-size recedes (G-35 * 2) · dual shelves as two tokens · NOT FOUND dims · "+N past the cap" honesty · CVD-safe kit pair untouched.
