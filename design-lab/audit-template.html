The rule, in one line: a part named only after a + on a Sales Info line ships inside that parent, so it leaves the parts total, the buildable count and the pick list. A part with its own line and its own MPN is real and stays.

Ties always resolve to keep. Wrongly dropping a part would under-price the kit and stop the picker fetching it — the box ships incomplete. Wrongly keeping one costs a wasted walk, caught at the bench.
